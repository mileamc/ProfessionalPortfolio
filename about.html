About Milena Caldas

A little about me

I was a quiet, creative kid, usually somewhere between the pages of a book and a drawing I was making. I love writing, reading, music, and spending time in nature. I also play board games and RPGs with friends, which is probably just another way of saying I enjoy getting lost in imaginary worlds.

What I do

I studied Graphic Design at UFPR and later joined the Apple Developer Academy, where I got to design apps alongside developers. After graduating, I joined Brazil's largest pediatric hospital as a Graphic Designer, where I've recently been rediscovering the meaning behind what I do and what I love about design.

I'm currently pursuing a postgraduate degree in UX Design, exploring the parts of design I enjoy most: thinking, ideating, and experimenting with new ideas before the pixels.

What I'm looking for

I'm interested in Product Design, Human-Computer Interaction, and Learning Design, particularly when technology can help create meaningful experiences for people. I want to keep exploring ideas, asking better questions, and building things that are worth making.

I'm currently based in Curitiba, Paraná, Brazil, where I was born and raised. I'm also open to remote positions or relocating for the right opportunity.
